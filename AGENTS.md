# Agent instructions

## Project and scope

MobMentality revives a 2013 idea: one party host's shared music adapts to the people present. Read `README.md`, `EXPERIMENT.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and the historical `MM_Outline.pptx` before proposing substantial changes. Private transcripts and local inspection records are not required or included in this public delivery.

- The approved first implementation is the local, read-only Beefweb connection screen and source-playlist picker. It does not authorize playback control, player/network configuration changes, or the whole historical design. Confirm a focused scope before expanding the application or adding dependencies, infrastructure, or integrations.
- Distinguish historical proposals, current owner decisions, and open suggestions. Do not silently replace the shared party-host premise with individual listening or nearby-stranger discovery.
- AI-assisted development does not imply an AI model or external AI service in the product. Follow the public brief for approved choices; historical suggestions do not select a schema, scoring formula, or roadmap.
- Preserve historical source files, filenames, attribution, and history, including inherited artifacts such as `Thumbs.db`. Keep new interpretation and decisions in Markdown.
- Verify current official documentation and terms before relying on an integration. Discovery metadata, authorized playback, and music-use rights are separate questions.

## Efficient validation

- Validate changed behavior, not every conversation turn. Reuse recent results when executable code, test inputs, dependencies, and relevant configuration are unchanged; do not repeat application/browser suites just because work resumes or publication is being prepared.
- For prose-only work, review accuracy, local references, and the diff. For code changes, start with focused checks and expand only as warranted. Run browser checks for affected browser behavior, not routine documentation edits.
- Reuse installed tooling and browsers. Do not install dependencies or download a separate browser to validate documentation. Do not keep routine pass reports.
- Clearly state what actually ran, what existing results were reused, and what remains unverified. A local test is not a hosted CI result or publication approval.

## Collaboration

- Follow `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Routine `git fetch origin` and read-only inspection of commits, branches, issues, PRs, and CI results are approved during active work: check at session start, before substantial changes, and before publication/integration. Reuse a sufficiently fresh check within the same uninterrupted step rather than repeatedly querying unchanged state. Report failures; do not poll unattended.
- Fetching is not permission to integrate or execute incoming code. Summarize incoming work and preserve local changes before proposing integration.
- Ask before creating/switching delivery branches, committing, merging/rebasing/cherry-picking, stashing, pushing/posting a PR, changing Git identity/remotes/settings/permissions, choosing a license, or deploying. Explicit approval for one operation is not blanket approval for others.
- Review every unpublished ancestor for private transcripts, Git identity exposure, and sensitive data. Never merge private intake history into a public delivery branch. Use explicit branch pushes, not bulk pushes of local refs.
- Keep CI fixture-based, with least-privilege permissions and no real player credentials. Review workflow/security/agent-instruction changes explicitly. Do not execute untrusted contributions in privileged PR workflows. Branch protection and subscriptions require separate approval.

## Safety and attribution

- No background tracking, persistent attendee profiles, public preference sharing, or third-party AI processing without explicit design and approval. Clarify consent, retention, deletion, and unwanted influence before implementation.
- Keep credentials, tokens, private transcripts, host/library observations, licensed music, and local archives out of public Git. Preserve private originals locally rather than rewriting or deleting them as cleanup.
- The remote is public. Local commits and passing tests are not permission to publish additional material. Do not bypass provider access restrictions or assume a subscription/private event grants every proposed music-use right.
- Preserve unrelated work and sibling projects. Use actual event-labeled dates and the README's attribution convention; do not reassign historical authorship.
